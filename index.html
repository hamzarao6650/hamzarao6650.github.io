Cutting damage on fragile large items across 27 facilities

AMXL, Amazon's large-item fulfillment network, was absorbing about $16.7M a year in damage concessions, and the drivers were underexplored. My goal was a 5 to 10% reduction with a fix that could scale to every site. I ran it as a 12-week DMAIC program.

  1. DDefine
    Scoped $16.7M in concessions across 27 facilities and set the KPIs.
  2. MMeasure
    Pulled data from several sources and split defects by category, path, shift, day and time.
  3. AAnalyze
    Pareto, 5-Why and Gemba walks at my site and other network sites.
  4. IImprove
    Two-week pilot of visual aids, training and revised standard work with Ops, Quality, Learning and WHS.
  5. CControl
    A 6-page narrative and a network scaling playbook, with the tools handed over.

Ruyi: taking a GenAI care robot for dementia from concept to clinical pilots

Ruyi is a 3-foot care companion robot that tracks mobility, talks with residents and keeps caregivers informed. I owned the roadmap, PRDs and releases across 30+ engineers, designers, AI specialists and clinicians in 3 countries, and launched 5 deployments with UCI, CWRU, Judson, Notre Dame and Mayo Clinic.

Adopt, do not build

A custom conversational model meant 6 to 8 months. I chose an open-source LLM and shipped a voice agent on NVIDIA edge AI in 2 months, tested on 1,000+ conversations against a clinician-built empathy rubric at 2-second latency.

Privacy by design

Edge processing, anonymized cloud outputs on AWS S3 and skeletal tracking in place of raw video. The study survey showed 100% positive privacy comfort.

Scope with 40+ experts

Brought NaviGait, Xavor, UCI Nursing and Judson Senior Living together, ranked features on capability, technical limits and user value, and locked scope with written sign-offs.

  • Helped win the UC Noyce research grant with UC Irvine and advance the NIH-funded AITC pilot with Case Western Reserve University at Judson Senior Living.
  • Cut pre-onboarding critical tickets 17% with site-readiness SOPs, SLAs, quality gates and 48-hour living-lab dry runs.
  • Cut component lead time from 45 to 21 days with a PO tracker, an approval workflow and vendor escalation across China, the U.S. and Pakistan.
  • Co-authored a peer-reviewed paper in JMIR Research Protocols (2024).
